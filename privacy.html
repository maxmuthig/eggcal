Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

  • EggCal runs on your computer and has no server. Nothing is sent to the developer.
  • It reads and writes the calendars and events of the accounts you connect, only to show and edit them in the app.
  • Calendar data and sign-in tokens are stored only on your computer. Delete the EggCal data folder to remove them.
  • No data is shared with third parties, no analytics, no advertising.
  • Google data: EggCal uses the Google Calendar scope and follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
